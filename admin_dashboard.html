Orders Information & WhatsApp Workflow

Direct serverless ordering dispatch architecture

How Order Processing Works in Grocery Gallery:

When a customer clicks ORDER NOW on the storefront, enters their name and delivery address, and confirms their order, the order details are dynamically constructed into a pre-formatted WhatsApp message containing the product name, PKR total calculation, and customer address.

The order is dispatched directly to the WhatsApp number associated with that product. This provides zero backend delay, maximum customer privacy, and direct instant communication!
